### 🔭 About Bitlyx
Bitlyx is a community-driven hub for high-quality scripts, automation systems, and modular projects. Our goal is to provide developers with a central library of battle-tested code.

### 📂 What's Inside?
* ⚙️ **Automation Systems** — From TG bots to workflow managers.
* 🛠 **Developer Utilities** — Data converters, log analyzers, and CLI tools.
* 🏗 **Project Blueprints** — Ready-to-use boilerplates for your next SaaS or App.
* 🧠 **AI Experiments** — Custom prompts and LLM integration scripts.

### 🤝 Community & Collaboration
All development, brainstorming, and support happen inside our Discord server. Join to:
